## Overview

An advertiser buys a slot on a radio station and then has to take the station's word for it that the spot ran. Tag Pro closes that gap: it monitors channels as they broadcast, verifies that the spots a client paid for actually aired, and reports back where and when each one did.

A three-month freelance engagement for Vertex Era, building the front end of that platform.

## My role

Sole front-end developer for the engagement. Worked directly with the client on the interface, picked the stack, and shipped to production at [tagpro.ae](https://www.tagpro.ae/).

## Approach

- Built the dashboard around **live status** — channel widgets and data visualisations that update while a broadcast is still running, so an operator sees a problem during the campaign rather than in a report afterwards
- Kept the audio player as a top-level component that survives route changes, so monitoring a live channel doesn't stop the moment you navigate to a different screen
- Used a global audio context, so any screen can control playback without prop-drilling through the page tree
- Made the whole product work at 320px as well as it does on a desktop browser, because the people checking a campaign are usually not sitting at a desk
- Lazy-loaded and virtualised the channel list once the catalogue outgrew a single screen
